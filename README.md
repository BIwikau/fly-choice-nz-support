Fly Choice NZ helps anglers choose practical fly-fishing setups for Taupō-region rivers based on river conditions, fishing depth, season and preferred fishing method.

## About Fly Choice NZ

Fly Choice NZ includes fly and setup recommendations, river information, live data for supported rivers, a personal fly box, saved fishing gear and catch-photo features.

River and weather information can change and should be used as a guide alongside current conditions and your own judgement.

## Privacy

Fly Choice NZ does not require an account or sign-in. Personal fishing gear, fly box information and saved setups are stored for use within the app.
