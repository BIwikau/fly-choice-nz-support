# Fly Choice NZ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Fly Choice NZ respects your privacy. The app is designed to provide fly-fishing recommendations, river information and fishing tools without requiring you to create an account.

## Information We Collect

Fly Choice NZ does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address or other personal account information.

Information you enter into the app, including saved fishing gear, fly box information and fishing preferences, is stored locally on your device.

## Camera and Photos

Fly Choice NZ includes an optional Photo Your Catch feature.

Camera and photo-library access is only used when you choose to take, select or save a catch photo. Your photos are not uploaded to Fly Choice NZ or stored on our servers.

## River and Weather Data

Fly Choice NZ accesses third-party river and weather information to provide current fishing conditions. Requests may be made to external data providers when these features are used.

Fly Choice NZ does not use this information to track your location.

## Analytics and Advertising

Fly Choice NZ does not include third-party advertising and does not use your personal information for advertising or tracking.

## Data Sharing

Fly Choice NZ does not sell your personal information.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the features or data practices of Fly Choice NZ change.
